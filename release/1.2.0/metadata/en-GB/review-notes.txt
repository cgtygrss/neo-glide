No account or sign-in is required to play.

Tap Play, then hold BOOST to rise and release it to descend. FIRE shoots at enemy ships. Garage previews are available without unlocking an item.

The game uses optional rewarded ads for an additional flight-credit reward or one continue. Cancelling an ad does not grant a reward.

Purchase and restore flows must be verified against the App Store Sandbox before submission. Do not submit this draft note until that verification is complete.
