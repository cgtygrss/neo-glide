# Flight earnings illustration

Illustrative model, not player telemetry. Uses actual trail generation at normal scroll (10 pixels per meter), no blocked spawns or time slow, and assumes spawned coins are reachable. Half collection is modeled as half the total face value, not a particular coin selection. Low multiplier is 1×; high is the theoretical 2× upgrade and 2× combo cap sustained for every collected coin. Distance pays 10 per meter. Excludes level/mission/ad rewards and purchases. A boss adds 10,000 when shown.
