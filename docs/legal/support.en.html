Support & information for parents

Updated: 12 November 2026

Neo Glide by Çağatay Gürses. Contact cgty5960@gmail.com with the game version, device/OS and the problem. Do not send passwords, card details or unnecessary information about a child.

Family play

Version 1.3.0, iOS build 12 / Android version code 5, introduces local age selection. Under 18 and unknown age selections have local gameplay without advertising, account, cloud event or real-money purchase requests. Earn credits in ordinary flights for ships, equipment and visual kits. Adult selection is not parental consent for a child. Use family play on shared devices used by children; update older versions before a child plays.

The age choice stays on the device. A younger selection cannot be upgraded through the game. A parent can contact support about mistakes; do not send a child's identity documents. Reinstalling or clearing app data can erase progress and is not a recommended correction process.

How to play and age suitability

Tap Play, hold BOOST to rise and release to glide. Use FIRE to shoot; pause for a break. The game includes fictional space combat and lasers. Store content ratings vary by region and are separate from the game's privacy age bands. A content rating is not parental consent.

Adult purchases and recovery

Permanent purchases are recognized automatically when the adult account and store are available. For a missing item, open Garage → Store → Missing a purchase? → Restore purchases, with the purchasing store account. Pending purchases unlock only after approval. Consumable credit packs are delivered once through the original verified transaction and are not repeatedly granted on restore. Give support the product and transaction reference, never card details.

Billing/refunds: Apple purchase support or Google Play refunds. The store decides refund eligibility. Game-account deletion does not itself request a refund.

Adult cloud progress

Home → Pilot → Account shows connection/save status. Reconnect the same Google or Game Center account to recover that pilot; configured Android builds use Google Play Games for platform identity. Review both save summaries before resolving a conflict. Family progress stays on the device.

Deletion and privacy

Use the in-game option or request account/data deletion without the app. Parents may request deletion of a child's previous online account. Rewarded ads are optional for adults and unavailable in family play. Adults can use the game's Ad privacy entry when available, iOS Tracking or Android advertising controls. Read the privacy policy.

Sound, accessibility and licenses

Settings and pause provide separate music/effects mute and volume controls. Reduced motion follows supported device preferences. Read accessibility information and software/music notices. The game includes offline legal documents in Settings.
