Accessibility in Neo Glide

Neo Glide is a landscape arcade flying game for iPhone and iPad. This page explains its presentation, controls and available adjustments so you can decide whether the experience suits your needs.

A dark interface

The game uses a dark interface by default, including the home screen, Garage, pilot screens, settings, flight, pause and results. There is no separate theme switch to turn on.

Sound and movement

Music and sound effects have separate switches and volume controls in Settings and during pause. The opening animation can be skipped by tapping. The iOS Reduce Motion preference shortens the opening and reduces selected decorative animations. Flight scrolling and combat effects remain part of gameplay.

Playing and pausing

Tap Play to begin. Hold BOOST to rise and release to glide. Use FIRE to shoot. The pause button stops a flight and lets you adjust sound before continuing. Ships, weapons and galaxies can be inspected in the Garage before unlocking them.

What to expect

The action relies on visual tracking and timed touch input. Full gameplay support with VoiceOver, Voice Control and text enlarged to 200% has not been verified. The App Store accessibility declaration currently covers the dark interface on iPhone and iPad.
