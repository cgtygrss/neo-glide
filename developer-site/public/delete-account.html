Delete your Neo Glide account and data

Developer: Çağatay Gürses · Updated: 12 November 2026

You can request deletion without installing the game. Parents/guardians may request deletion of a child's previous online account. Current family play does not create a cloud account.

Request deletion by email

  1. Email cgty5960@gmail.com, subject Neo Glide account deletion.
  2. Say you want the account and associated cloud data deleted. Include the sign-in provider, pilot name and, if available, Account reference from the game's account screen. For Google, use the linked email when possible. Parents should identify themselves as parent/guardian without unnecessary child details.
  3. We may request a proportionate ownership check to protect other players. Never send passwords, authentication codes, card details or government ID. If an anonymous account cannot be identified, we explain the limitation instead of deleting another person's data.

The request is submitted when you send the email; clicking the link alone does not submit it. We respond within applicable legal deadlines, normally within 30 days after receiving a valid request, and explain any legally permitted extension.

Delete in the app

Adult online account: Home → Pilot → Account → Manage account → Delete account → Delete permanently. A recent sign-in may be required. Family players should ask a parent to use the email route for an earlier cloud account. Current local family progress can be removed through device app-storage controls.

Deleted and retained information

Deletion removes the game authentication account, cloud save, pilot name, equipment/ownership and event progress, associated reward intents/leases and player/account-merge records. A hashed closed-account marker and limited transaction replay-prevention records may remain for security, duplicate-grant prevention, disputes or legal obligations; retained purchase records no longer contain the game account UID. Support messages remain while needed for the request or legal obligations.

Provider backups, service/security logs and platform billing records follow their providers' retention. Deletion does not delete your Apple, Google or Game Center account, automatically refund purchases or remove local copies/backups on other devices. See the privacy policy.
