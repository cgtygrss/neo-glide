# Neo Glide privacy policy

The maintained iOS/Android policy is generated from
[docs/legal/privacy.en.html](docs/legal/privacy.en.html) by `npm run legal:build`.

- Public policy: https://neo-glide-live-developer.web.app/privacy.html
- Account/data deletion: https://neo-glide-live-developer.web.app/delete-account.html
- In-app offline copy: Settings → Privacy policy

The game uses local storage and, for adult online features, Firebase, platform
identity/store services and optional AdMob advertising. The superseded 2025
“no data / no third-party services” template must not be reused.

Family play is introduced in iOS 1.3.0 (12) / Android 1.3.0 (5); older releases do
not include its age selection and restrictions. See the commercial release
checklist for deployment and remaining jurisdiction-specific review.
