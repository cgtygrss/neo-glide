Privacy policy

Updated: 12 November 2026

Neo Glide is developed by Çağatay Gürses, the controller for the game's own services. Contact cgty5960@gmail.com. This policy covers iOS, Android, the developer website and support.

Age groups and family play

Version 1.3.0, iOS build 12 / Android version code 5, introduces an age-group choice stored only on the device. We do not ask for a birthday or send the age group to a server. Under 18 and unknown-age players use family play: the game does not start advertising requests, tracking prompts, account sign-in, cloud synchronization, online events or real-money purchases. Progress and earned items stay on the device. Family legal screens do not open external websites or email applications.

Older versions do not include these protections: parents should update before a child plays. Selecting adult is not parental consent for a child. Use family play on a shared device used by children. A younger selection cannot be upgraded in the game; contact support about an incorrect selection. Parents can request deletion of a child's earlier online account.

Local information

The game stores progress, credits, equipment, appearances, sound preferences, the age choice and limited purchase/reward records on the device to run and save the game. Family play does not upload them to our services. Operating-system backups and platform services depend on your device settings and Apple or Google. Uninstalling or clearing app data may remove local progress; device backups may retain copies.

Google Play builds also include Google's automatic installer check to help verify that the app was installed through Google Play. It normally works offline; if the Play Store has been offline for a long time, checks can periodically need a connection. This platform protection is independent of our local age choice and game account/cloud-save services. Read Google Play's automatic protection information and Google's Privacy Policy.

Adult accounts and cloud services

For an adult selection, the game can restore a session or create an anonymous Firebase account. Optional Game Center, Google sign-in and configured Google Play Games connections process provider identifiers and supplied profile information, such as a display name and, for Google sign-in, email. The game does not request phone numbers, contact lists, precise location, camera or microphone access.

Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions process account identifiers, pilot names, progress, balances, equipment, ownership, events and verification records to synchronize saves, deliver rewards, recover purchases and protect the service. Providers also receive technical request information such as IP addresses and service logs. We do not integrate Firebase Analytics for gameplay analytics. A general SDK declaration does not establish that the game collects every data type the SDK can process.

Adult purchases

Apple handles App Store payments; Google handles Google Play payments. We do not receive card details. Product IDs, signed transactions or purchase tokens, transaction identifiers and ownership history are processed to verify and deliver purchases, restore permanent items, handle refunds/revocations and prevent duplicate grants. Credits can only be spent in Neo Glide, cannot be withdrawn for cash and cannot be transferred to other players.

Optional adult advertising

Adults may choose rewarded Google AdMob ads. Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) manages regional choices. Depending on choices and permissions, Google and advertising partners may process device/advertising identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, ad activity, app interactions, crashes, performance and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Personalization and tracking require the applicable permissions and consent. Ordinary play and earned-credit unlocks do not require an ad or tracking permission.

Read Google's Privacy Policy, information from partner apps and advertising information. The applicable UMP message identifies the advertising partners relevant to your choices.

Legal grounds and choices

Where GDPR or similar rules apply, information needed for requested adult account, cloud and purchase features is processed to perform those services. Security and duplicate-payment prevention rely on legitimate interests in protecting players and the service, subject to your rights. Records required by law rely on legal obligations. Advertising personalization, optional tracking and storage requiring consent rely on consent. Support information is processed to respond to service and rights requests. Essential local saving provides the game you request.

Adults can decline or withdraw applicable advertising choices through Settings → Ad privacy when UMP makes that form available. Change iOS tracking permission in device Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Android advertising controls are in the device's Google/Ads settings; names vary by OS. Withdrawal affects future processing, without undoing earlier lawful processing. Contact support for other privacy requests. You can always choose not to request an ad.

Recipients and processing locations

Recipients include Google/Firebase for authentication/hosting; Google and disclosed ad partners for adult advertising; Apple/Game Center for iOS identity/store services; and Google Play/Google sign-in for Android identity/store services. Providers also process certain information independently under their policies. We do not sell our cloud-save database to data brokers.

Firestore and Cloud Functions are configured in europe-west3 (Germany). Authentication, advertising, support and platform services can process information in other countries, including the United States. Provider processing and transfer provisions are described in Firebase privacy information and Google Cloud processing terms. Those terms do not replace requirements applying to us, including local transfer requirements. Contact us for information about the providers, locations and safeguards relevant to your request.

Retention and deletion

Local saves remain until removed or replaced on the device. Cloud progress is retained while the account is maintained, until deletion is requested or it is no longer needed for the service. Deletion removes the active account and associated cloud/player records. Limited transaction replay-prevention records and a hashed closed-account marker can remain to prevent fraud or repeat grants, resolve disputes or meet legal requirements. Retained purchase records no longer contain the game account UID. Provider backups, operational/security logs and store records follow provider retention practices and applicable law.

Adults can delete an online account in Home → Pilot → Account → Manage account → Delete account, or use account deletion without the app. Parents can request deletion of a child's earlier online account there. Deletion does not itself refund purchases or erase independently maintained Apple/Google records.

Depending on law, you can request information/access and a copy, correction, deletion, restriction or portability; object to legitimate-interest processing; withdraw consent; and complain to your competent data-protection authority. Under Türkiye's KVKK you may also ask whether data is processed, learn its purposes and recipients, request correction/erasure and their notification to recipients, object to adverse results of exclusively automated analysis and seek compensation for unlawful processing. Email cgty5960@gmail.com. We verify ownership proportionately, without routinely requesting passwords, card details or government identification, and respond within applicable legal deadlines.

Website, support and changes

Adult online mode checks a public version file hosted by GitHub. That hosting provider receives the technical web request, such as an IP address; no game account, save or age-group value is included in that request. Family play does not perform this check.

Our developer website adds no analytics or advertising scripts. Firebase Hosting can process technical requests/security logs. Email providers handle support messages; we retain correspondence while needed for the request, dispute or legal obligations. Do not send unnecessary information about a child. We update this policy as services change and present any new choice required before affected processing. Contact cgty5960@gmail.com.
